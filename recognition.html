Reviews & Recognition

A record of the attention James Mulhern’s work has received, in the words of those who offered it.

James Mulhern writes fiction and poetry of uncommon tenderness and precision. His novels Give Them Unquiet Dreams and Molly Bonamici, his novella A Prayer for Home, and his story collection Assumptions and Other Stories have drawn the attention of some of publishing’s most exacting readers, and his poems have appeared in more than one hundred literary journals and anthologies.

Recognition

Kirkus Reviews named Give Them Unquiet Dreams one of the Best Books of 2019, honoring the novel with a starred review — the publication’s highest distinction.

  • Kirkus ReviewsBest Books of 2019 (Indie), for Give Them Unquiet Dreams (starred review)
  • The Wishing Shelf Book Awards2020 Finalist, Adult Fiction, for Give Them Unquiet Dreams and Molly Bonamici
  • Aesthetica Creative Writing Award2020 Shortlist, Poetry, for “Copacetic” (published in the Creative Writing Anthology 2021)
  • Fish Publishing Short Story Prize2015 Longlist, for “A Nun’s Arse”
  • Best of the Net2025 Nomination, for “Honeymoon”
  • Short Story America PrizeTwo Honorable Mentions (2015) — “A Prayer for Home” was among only 31 stories selected for the Short Story America anthology that year
  • Readers’ Favorite5-star reviews for three of his books
