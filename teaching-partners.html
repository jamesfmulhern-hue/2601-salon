Who It's For

Adult readers and writers looking for serious, discussion-based literary programming · retirement-community and senior-living residents · libraries and bookstores building an author-talk or course series · universities and lifelong-learning programs · literary nonprofits and festivals seeking a credentialed, Kirkus-recognized instructor · secondary schools booking a writing or literary-analysis session for students.

How It's Delivered

Where. Your venue — a room the host provides. Philadelphia region; elsewhere in the Northeast on request.
Length. Single evening to sixteen-session series, shaped to your calendar.
Materials. Syllabus, discussion questions, and readings supplied — all public-domain or open-access, so students never buy a book.
Cost. A first session is often offered without charge as a proof of concept. Ongoing terms fit the host.

How to Say Yes

A short email is enough — your venue, an approximate audience size, and a proposed date window. A reply comes within two business days with a shape that fits.
