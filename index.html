The 2601 Salon

Free Courses

A literary salon for adult readers and writers, taught by James F. Mulhern — Professor of English and recipient of a Writing Fellowship at Exeter College, University of Oxford. Professor Mulhern is also a published novelist, short story writer, poet, and essayist.

The Courses

Click any course to see its full syllabus, readings, and sessions.

Survey of American Literature
Sixteen Sessions ›

A chronological journey from Indigenous voices and Puritan sermons to contemporary American writing — read slowly, together.

Women in American Literature
Sixteen Sessions ›

From Anne Bradstreet and Phillis Wheatley to Toni Morrison and Joy Harjo — a chronological reading of the women whose work makes the American canon whole.

Minority Voices in American Literature
Sixteen Sessions ›

Black, Latinx, Asian American, Native American, and immigrant voices — from Frederick Douglass to Ocean Vuong — read with the close attention they have always deserved.

Jewish Writers: A Literary Tradition
Sixteen Sessions ›

Sholem Aleichem, Kafka, Singer, Primo Levi, Celan, Bellow, Malamud, Ozick, Paley, Amichai — a literature older than empires, read together.

The Irish Literary Imagination
Sixteen Sessions ›

Sixteen weeks of Irish writing — from the Táin and Swift to Yeats, Joyce, Heaney, Boland, and the contemporary moment. Personal territory for your instructor.

Literature & American History — A Cross-Reading
Sixteen Sessions ›

Each week pairs a primary historical document with the literary work answering it — the Declaration with Douglass, the 14th Amendment with Du Bois, the Pentagon Papers with O'Brien.

Reading Poetry: A Beginner's Welcome
Sixteen Sessions ›

An unhurried introduction to poetry for anyone who has ever felt locked out of it. We read aloud, we sit with the lines, and we discover that a poem is not a code to crack.

Shakespeare's Sonnets & Famous Speeches
Sixteen Sessions ›

Sixteen sonnets paired with sixteen of Shakespeare's most famous monologues — short, self-contained texts read aloud and discussed. No full plays, no homework dread.

The Personal Essay & Memoir
Sixteen Sessions ›

Writing from a life — your own. We read masters of the form and we write by hand, in scene, with candor and craft.

The Craft of Revision — A Writers' Workshop
Sixteen Sessions ›

Bring your pages. We will read them aloud, cut them, sharpen them, and send them out.

The Writing Life: Getting Published
Sixteen Sessions ›

The literary magazine scene, cover letters and queries, agents, small presses, contests — the practical work of becoming a published writer, taught by a working writer and former editor.

The Art of Telling
Ten Sessions ›

A salon in short story, poetry, and memoir. Ten Sundays across the three great narrative forms — the course that gave the salon its name, now on the shelf beside the others.

About This Salon

The 2601 Salon is a free literary program of Silver Current Press — a working catalog of the courses Professor Mulhern is prepared to teach for adult readers and writers. Every course meets weekly for ninety minutes. There are no grades, no quizzes, no fees, and no prerequisites — only an invitation to sit with serious books, to talk about them in plain language, and to improve one's own writing along the way. These courses are about both literature and the craft of writing.

Each course is shaped the same way: a brief historical framing at the top of the hour, a passage read aloud, a guided discussion around four to six open questions, and an optional ten-minute writing prompt at the close. Any device with a browser suffices for in-class access to the readings. Writing in class, however, is always done by hand. We have found this matters.

Readings are free. Most are in the public domain and are linked from each course page.

About the Instructor

James F. Mulhern is a Philadelphia novelist and Professor Emeritus of English. His novel Give Them Unquiet Dreams received a Kirkus starred review and was named a Kirkus Best Book of 2019. He held a fully funded Writing Fellowship at Exeter College, University of Oxford; was nominated for a Pushcart Prize; and was shortlisted for the Aesthetica Creative Writing Award. He served as Department Chair in both English and Social Studies and as an AP Consultant for the College Board and the National Math and Science Initiative. He is the founder of Silver Current Press, a Philadelphia literary imprint. Full biography at authorjamesmulhern.com.

Formats & Availability

Courses are offered in three formats, and Professor Mulhern will adapt any of the courses in this catalog to fit a partner venue's calendar and audience.

  • In person — at libraries, bookstores, community centers, universities, retirement communities, and literary nonprofits in the Philadelphia region and, on request, elsewhere in the Northeast.
  • Online — by Zoom or comparable platform, to any group of adult readers anywhere.
  • Hybrid — a combination of in-person sessions and online continuation, tailored to the host.

Full sixteen-session courses, four-session workshops, single evening talks, and one-day masterclasses are all available. Please write with your calendar and I will propose a shape that fits.

How to Enroll or Book a Course

Individual learners — if you would like to attend a course, express interest in a future session, or receive occasional announcements about new offerings, please write to me at jamesfmulhern@gmail.com. There is no formal enrollment.

Program directors, librarians, and venue partners — if you host adult programming and would like to bring a course to your community, please write to jamesfmulhern@gmail.com. The first offering at any new venue is free as a proof of concept; ongoing engagements are arranged at a modest honorarium. Please include the venue, an approximate audience size, and a proposed date window.
