Section IWhat I Can Do for Your Audience

I am a Philadelphia novelist and professor emeritus of English with more than three decades of classroom experience — undergraduate seminars, graduate-level workshops, secondary-school classes for AP students, and a fully funded Writing Fellowship at Exeter College, University of Oxford. I have chaired departments in both English and Social Studies. My novel Give Them Unquiet Dreams received a Kirkus starred review and was named a Kirkus Best Book of 2019. I am the founder of Silver Current Press, a literary imprint based here in Philadelphia.

What I bring to a partnership is a body of literature-and-craft courses designed for adult readers who are not looking for a degree. Every course is discussion-based, generous, close in its reading, and non-competitive. I do not lecture; I open the material and let the room work with it. The best sessions I have ever taught — including the ones that led directly to the salon you are reading — have not happened in seminar rooms. They have happened wherever a serious group of adults sat down together with a shared text.

When a course lands well at a new venue, I am glad to offer the first offering without charge as a proof of concept for both of us. Ongoing terms are shaped to fit the host. This is intended to make it easy for a program partner to say yes.

Section IIIAbout the Instructor

James F. Mulhern
Novelist · Professor Emeritus of English · Oxford Writing Fellow · Founder of Silver Current Press

James F. Mulhern has spent his working life inside two disciplines that reinforce each other: the disciplined reading of literature, and the disciplined making of it. He taught English for more than three decades — in secondary and college classrooms, as a department chair, and as a mentor to writers at every level from apprentice to publishing professional. His novels and short fiction have received a Kirkus Starred Review, a place on Kirkus’s Best Books of 2019 list, a Pushcart Prize nomination in fiction, and an Aesthetica Creative Writing Award shortlist in poetry. In 2015 Exeter College at the University of Oxford awarded him a fully funded writing fellowship, an unusually competitive honor for a mid-career American writer.

He is the author of seven books — Give Them Unquiet Dreams, Molly Bonamici, The Weight of Small Mercies, All We Ever Needed, Crossings, and companion volumes in publishing and craft — available on Amazon and through Silver Current Press. Since retiring from full-time teaching, he has directed his practice toward what he considers the most useful next act for a writer with his years and credentials: teaching adults to read literature and to write it, together, in the format that first made him love the work — the salon.

He lives and works in Philadelphia. The courses in this catalog are the courses he most wants to teach and the ones he has been teaching, in one form or another, for years. Bringing them to a partner venue is not a favor to him — it is the shape of his continuing practice.

Section VIHow I Can Deliver a Course

In person. At libraries, bookstores, community centers, universities, retirement communities, and literary nonprofits — anywhere in the Philadelphia region, and, on request, elsewhere in the Northeast.
Formats. Full sixteen-session series, four-session workshops, single evening author talks, or one-day masterclasses.

What I supply. Syllabus, discussion questions, close-reading passages, revision checklists, and — if useful — a live classroom whiteboard at whiteboard.silvercurrentpress.com for shared writing during sessions. All readings are drawn from the public domain or open-access sources; students never need to buy a book.

What the host supplies. A room and its promotion. Everything else is on my side.

How to Say Yes

The simplest way to explore a partnership is a short email — the venue, an approximate audience size, and a proposed date window. I will reply within two business days with a shape that fits, a proposed syllabus, and the details of what a first offering would look like.
